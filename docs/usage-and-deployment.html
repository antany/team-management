Team Manager

Usage guide and deployment notes

What it is

A private tool for one manager: your team, who is away and when, who works on what, and notes to follow up on. Later it groups what you wrote into a summary that helps you write performance reviews. Your data belongs to your account only. It works offline and syncs when you are back online.

Signing in

  • Create an account with a username and password (8+ characters). No email is stored, so a forgotten password cannot be reset.
  • Install it from the browser menu ("Install app" / "Add to Home Screen") to use it like an app.

Protecting sensitive notes

  • Set a PIN (Home banner or Settings, 4 to 8 digits, 6+ recommended). The notes cached on your device are then encrypted with it.
  • Auto-lock: after 2 minutes without activity (change in Settings) the app returns to Home and asks for the PIN. It also locks the moment you switch to another app or tab. The lock button in the top bar locks immediately.
  • Reloading the page always locks. While locked, nothing from the app is on screen or in memory.
  • Five wrong PINs sign you out and clear this device. Your data is safe on the server; sign in again to get it back.
  • Forgot the PIN? Use "Forgot PIN? Sign out of this device", then sign in with your password.
  • The PIN never leaves the device and cannot be recovered. It protects against a casual look or a lost phone, not a determined attacker who copies the device's storage.

Using it

Team

Add people with a role, contact details (email, phone, emergency contact; tap to call or write) and notes about them. Archive someone who leaves; their history stays for reviews. Open a person to see their notes, leave and work.

Calendar

  • Add leave with the + button (vacation, sick, personal day, or other with a reason). Weekends and holidays inside a range are not counted.
  • Conflicts: a day with two or more people away is highlighted (orange); at your threshold (default 3, Settings) it turns red. Tap a day to see who is available and who is out, and why.
  • Switch to Week for a grid of people by weekday with a coverage row (for example 2/4).
  • "Heads-up" lists upcoming overlaps and leave that lands on someone who is allocated 80% or more.
  • Missing a holiday? Tap Add holiday (or "Mark this day as a holiday" on a day). It counts as a non-working day like the built-in ones, syncs to your devices and is included in backups. Edit or delete it from the day panel.
  • Holidays are pre-filled: Ontario statutory holidays plus Remembrance Day and the National Day for Truth and Reconciliation (federal / bank holidays). Each group can be switched off; Civic Holiday is optional and off by default. Weekend substitute days are not shown.

Work

Give each person a share of their time per project, with dates. The weekly grid shows the highest load of each week and flags anything over 100%.

Notes

Record 1:1s, feedback, kudos, concerns and goals. Give a note a follow-up date and it appears in Follow-ups (overdue / today / upcoming) and on Home. Tick it when done.

Review prep

Pick a person and a period. You get leave taken, each project's allocation while it was active, your notes grouped by kind, and follow-up counts. Copy it as Markdown, download it, or print it. It is built only from what you wrote, nothing is scored or sent anywhere, and you write the review yourself.

Backups and restore

  • With a PIN set, the app makes an encrypted JSON backup once a day while you use it and keeps the last 14 on the server. Settings has Back up now and Download encrypted file.
  • The backups are encrypted on your device with your PIN. The server (and anyone who gets the file) cannot read them, and the PIN is never sent anywhere.
  • If something goes wrong: Settings, Backup and restore, choose a backup (or Restore from file), enter the PIN that was set when it was made, then choose Replace (everything matches the backup) or Add missing (brings back what is in the backup, keeps the rest). A copy of your current data is downloaded first, so a restore can be undone.
  • Forgetting the PIN makes older backups unreadable. If you change the PIN, keep the old one until the old backups age out.

Sync and offline

Everything is saved on the device first and synced about two seconds later when online (chip in the top bar: Synced / Syncing / Offline). If two devices change the same item, the most recent edit wins. Signing out clears the device; if edits have not synced yet you are asked to confirm.

Deploying

./gradlew :backend:bootJar
podman build -f podman/Containerfile -t localhost/team-management:latest .
cp podman/secrets.example.yaml podman/secrets.yaml   # fill in, chmod 600
podman kube play podman/secrets.yaml podman/team-pod.yaml
  1. In Cloudflare Zero Trust create a tunnel, put its token in secrets.yaml, and point a public hostname at http://localhost:8081. The app is served under /team/.
  2. Create your account, then set APP_PASSWORD_SIGNUP to "false" in podman/team-pod.yaml and replay the pod so nobody else can register.
  3. Back up nightly with scripts/backup-db.sh from cron (keeps 14 days). Restore: gunzip -c team_<stamp>.sql.gz | podman exec -i team-postgres psql -U team team.
  4. Start the pod at boot with a systemd unit / quadlet.

The PIN encryption needs https (the tunnel provides it) or localhost. Over plain http the PIN cannot be set.
